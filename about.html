About The Keys Salt Scrub

The Keys Salt Scrub is handcrafted in small artisan batches right here in Tavernier, Florida. Our proprietary formula combines salts, coconut oil, and essential oils — all-natural and chemical-free.

Our philosophy is simple: if you keep your dollars close to home, you make a difference close to home. We’re proud to create locally-made products with quality ingredients at accessible pricing, stimulating the regional economy by keeping revenue circulating right here in the Florida Keys.

We’re proud members of the Key Largo Chamber of Commerce, Islamorada Chamber of Commerce, Florida Keys Marathon, and Lower Keys Chamber — supporting the community that supports us.
